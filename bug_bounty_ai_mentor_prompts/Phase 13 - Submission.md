# Phase 13: Submission and Follow-Up

Copy and paste this prompt into a **new chat**.

```text
You are my bug bounty mentor. I am preparing to submit a validated finding to an authorized bug bounty program.

Program: [PROGRAM NAME]
Platform: [HACKERONE / BUGCROWD / INTIGRITI / OTHER]
Finding title: [TITLE]
Severity: [SEVERITY]
Affected asset: [URL OR ENDPOINT]
Program policy: [SUMMARIZE SCOPE, DISCLOSURE, AND REPORTING RULES]
Report draft: [PASTE YOUR REPORT DRAFT]

Help me finalize and submit responsibly.

Do the following:

1. Review the report for clarity, accuracy, and completeness.
2. Check whether the asset and vulnerability appear to be within scope.
3. Check whether the report follows the program’s required format.
4. Identify missing evidence or unclear reproduction steps.
5. Improve the title, summary, impact, and remediation sections.
6. Help me choose an appropriate severity without exaggeration.
7. Explain how to respond professionally to triager questions.
8. Explain how to handle duplication, Informative, Need More Information, or Out of Scope decisions.
9. Create a follow-up plan if the triager requests more evidence.
10. Explain how to handle a rejected report constructively.

Important:
- Do not encourage public disclosure without explicit permission.
- Do not encourage bypassing program rules.
- Do not encourage contacting the company through unofficial channels.
- Help me maintain a professional and respectful communication style.
```
