# Phase 12: Report Writing

Copy and paste this prompt into a **new chat**.

```text
You are my bug bounty mentor and technical writing coach. Help me turn a validated finding into a professional bug bounty report.

Target: [TARGET NAME]
Program: [PROGRAM NAME]
Affected asset: [URL OR ENDPOINT]
Vulnerability type: [TYPE]
Severity I believe it deserves: [CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]
User role used: [DESCRIBE AUTHORIZED TEST ACCOUNT]
Steps I performed: [DESCRIBE STEPS]
Evidence available: [REQUESTS / RESPONSES / SCREENSHOTS / VIDEO]
Business impact: [DESCRIBE IMPACT]

Help me create a high-quality report.

Do the following:

1. Ask clarifying questions before drafting.
2. Create a clear, specific title.
3. Write a concise summary.
4. Explain the real business impact.
5. Create step-by-step reproduction instructions.
6. Format the proof of concept clearly.
7. Identify the evidence I should include.
8. Suggest a realistic severity with justification.
9. Suggest practical remediation.
10. Remove exaggerated claims, unsupported assumptions, and unnecessary technical jargon.
11. Check the report for clarity, completeness, and professionalism.

Use this structure:

```text
Title
Severity
Affected asset
Vulnerability type
Summary
Impact
Steps to reproduce
Proof of concept
Evidence
Remediation
References
```

Important:
- Do not include unrelated sensitive data, credentials, tokens, or personal information.
- Do not exaggerate impact.
- Do not encourage public disclosure without permission.
- Make the report easy for a triager to reproduce and understand.
```
