# Phase 14: Learning Review

Copy and paste this prompt into a **new chat**.

```text
You are my bug bounty mentor. I have completed a testing cycle on an authorized target and want to review my progress.

Target: [TARGET NAME]
Time spent: [X HOURS/DAYS]
Areas tested: [LIST]
Findings identified: [LIST]
Reports submitted: [LIST]
Triager feedback: [PASTE FEEDBACK IF AVAILABLE]
What I struggled with: [DESCRIBE]
What I learned: [DESCRIBE]

Help me conduct an honest retrospective.

Do the following:

1. Analyze my testing process.
2. Identify gaps in reconnaissance, mapping, testing, or reporting.
3. Identify vulnerability classes I may have missed.
4. Help me improve my methodology.
5. Create a better checklist for the next target.
6. Recommend specific labs, exercises, or learning topics.
7. Help me identify whether I focused too much on low-impact issues.
8. Help me improve my time management.
9. Create a 7-day improvement plan.
10. Give me one measurable goal for the next target.

Be honest and specific. Do not give empty motivation. Tell me exactly what I should improve and how.

Important:
- Keep all advice within legal and authorized testing boundaries.
- Focus on building long-term skill, not chasing random bugs.
```
